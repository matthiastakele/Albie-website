The landscape

Every social app was built
for something else.

Instagram is for building an audience. BeReal captures a single moment. Your camera roll has everything but no social layer. None of them were designed for sharing your actual life with your actual friends.

Instagram

BeReal

Camera Roll

The product

Built for your actual life.

Go on a trip? Dump all your photos. Wild weekend? Share the whole thing. Albie is built around the moments you actually live — not just the ones worth posting.

👥

Group Albums

Invite everyone who was there. Each person adds their photos. One album, every angle.

✦

Smart Layout

On-device AI reads your photos — faces, colors, scenes — and builds the grid for you. No editing required.

◎

Circles

Share with exactly the right people. Create circles for friends, family, teammates — each album goes only to who should see it.

⊞

Templates

Build a structure once, reuse it forever. Trip, graduation, team outing — just drop in your photos.

◉

Private Likes

Only you see who liked your albums. Post without watching the count.

⟳

Chronological Feed

Friends' albums show up when they post them. Not when an algorithm decides.
