The problem

You took 10,000 photos
last year.

Your friends saw 30.

The rest live in your camera roll forever. Not because they weren't worth sharing — but because nothing made it easy.

The landscape

Every social app was built
for something else.

Instagram is for building an audience. BeReal captures a single moment. Your camera roll has everything but no social layer. None of them were designed for sharing your actual life with your actual friends.

Instagram

Built for reach

You curate, edit, and post for an audience. The grid and the algorithm reward performance, not authenticity.

BeReal

Built for one moment

A random snapshot of your day is honest, but it only captures a sliver of what actually happened.

Camera Roll

Not social at all

You have hundreds of photos from the trip, the weekend, the season. No one ever sees them.

The Facebook Album captured something real. It died, and nothing replaced it.

The product

An album for every moment.
Shared with the right people.

Albie brings back the social album. Create one for a trip, a weekend, a season, or a random Tuesday. Drop all your photos in. Invite the friends who were there to add theirs.

📸

Up to 100 photos

Share the whole story, not just the best shot. Drop everything in and let the album breathe.

👥

Collaborative albums

Invite friends to contribute their photos. Every perspective from the same moment, all in one place.

🔁

Chronological feed

See what your friends are sharing in the order they posted it. No ranking, no suppression.

🙈

Private likes

Only you can see who liked your albums. Post freely without worrying about the count.

✨

Album templates

Save structures for recurring moments. "Weekend trip," "team outing," "family dinner" — reuse what works.
